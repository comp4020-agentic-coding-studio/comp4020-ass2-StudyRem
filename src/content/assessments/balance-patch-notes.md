## The brief

> Everyone has an opinion about which item is overpowered. Only some of
> those opinions survive being turned into a ratio.

Balance Sheet Clinic (Week 5) had you build a cost-effectiveness ratio from
data provided in the room and use it to argue for a rebalance out loud.
This assessment is the individually-marked, fuller version of the same
exercise, against a fresh dataset you haven't seen: a small set of
competing in-game items or ships, each with a currency cost and a
performance number (damage, defence, speed --- whatever the dataset
provides).

The numeric-balancing move from Week 5's lecture is the one being tested
here: performance-per-currency ratios expose which options are
underpriced or overpriced relative to their peers, and a rebalance is
just moving the outlier back toward the pack.

## What you submit

Real patch notes for one specific numeric change, backed by your working:

- the cost-effectiveness ratio you built, as a formula with the dataset's
  numbers substituted in, for every option in the set
- one option identified as the outlier, with its ratio compared against
  the pack
- one specific change (a new price, stat, or unlock requirement) and the
  recalculated ratio showing the outlier now sits within a reasonable band

Keep the tone of an actual patch note --- terse, numeric, and clear about
what changed and why --- rather than a discursive essay about balance in
general.
