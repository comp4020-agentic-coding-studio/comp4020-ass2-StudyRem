## Before the session

Have a draft of your intervention argument, even a rough one --- naming the
real economy, the specific problem, and the mechanism you're proposing. This
session is for stress-testing that draft, not starting it.

## In the session

Short, timed pitches to a small group of peers, followed by fast feedback
focused on one question: would a marker who's read the whole semester's
material find this specific and defensible?

- 5 minutes: format and timing rules
- 40 minutes: pitches and feedback in groups of four
- 10 minutes: whole-room debrief on common gaps

## Afterwards

The Economic Intervention Report is due at the end of this week. This
session is your last chance to hear "that's too vague" from a person
instead of from a mark.
