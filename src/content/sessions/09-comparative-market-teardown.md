## Before the session

Read Week 9's lecture and its comparison table. Pick, in advance, which two
of EVE Online, World of Warcraft, and Old School RuneScape you'll dig into
--- you won't have time to research a third during the session.

## In the session

Working in pairs, dig one level deeper than the lecture's table: find a
specific mechanic (not just "EVE is more transparent") and articulate why
the other game's designers plausibly rejected it.

- 10 minutes: pick a pair, confirm your two games
- 30 minutes: research and draft the comparison
- 15 minutes: present to the room

## Afterwards

This session's research skill --- finding a specific, real mechanic and
arguing why it exists --- is exactly what the Scandal Case Study Essay
(due this week) asks you to do for a documented market incident.
