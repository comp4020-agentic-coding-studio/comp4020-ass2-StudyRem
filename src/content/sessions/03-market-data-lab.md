## Before the session

Read Week 3's lecture on matched-order books versus listing markets. Bring a
laptop that can open a spreadsheet --- the dataset is provided, not built by
you.

## In the session

Marcus hands out a cleaned, anonymised trade-history slice (Grand-Exchange
style guide prices alongside the underlying trades that produced them). Work
in pairs to find where the two numbers disagree, and by how much.

- 10 minutes: orientation to the dataset's columns
- 25 minutes: pair work finding and explaining divergences
- 15 minutes: two pairs present a finding to the room

## Afterwards

The divergence-finding skill from this session is exactly what the Market
Data Report (due Week 4) asks you to repeat against a report you haven't
seen yet.
