Before Slop University, Sana spent six years as the resident economist on a
live-service MMO, where her job was to read a firehose of transaction logs
every week and decide whether the currency needed a new sink before players
noticed the inflation themselves. She left industry when she realised she was
having more fun explaining *why* a market broke than shipping the patch that
fixed it.

She teaches the framing weeks and the case studies, and marks the Capstone.
If a lecture cites a real number, she is the one who chased down where it came
from.
