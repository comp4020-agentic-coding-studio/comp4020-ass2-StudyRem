Lior builds agent-based models of markets for a living --- thousands of simple
trading agents, no single one of them smart, whose collective behaviour still
manages to reproduce the bubbles and crashes real economies produce. Week
10's lecture is their one appearance on the roster, walking through how the
same technique applies to a game economy with a few thousand real players
standing in for the agents.

Direct any Week 10 questions to Lior directly during that fortnight; outside
of it, ask Sana or Marcus instead.
