Marcus builds every dataset the studio sessions run on: cleaned-up market
histories, a toy currency-sink model that's just complicated enough to break
in an interesting way, the spreadsheet you'll be asked to patch in Week 6. He
also runs the sessions themselves, which means he's usually the first to
notice when a lecture's argument doesn't survive contact with real numbers.

Come to him first with anything about a session's dataset or a submission
format; go to Sana for anything about the course itself.
