# Glossary

The lectures, sessions and assessments lean on real terminology from the
course's three case-study games --- EVE Online, World of Warcraft, and Old
School RuneScape. This page defines each one in plain language. It doesn't
cover the course's own vocabulary (faucet, sink, and the like): those are
concepts the lectures introduce and define themselves, not borrowed jargon.

None of this course's assessments require you to have played any of these
games --- everything is explained here or cited from published sources. But
some of it is easier to feel than to read about. If you have the time, EVE
Online's core client is free to play, so a short session there costs
nothing but time; for World of Warcraft and Old School RuneScape, a
market-focused stream or a short "how the auction house works" video gets
most of the same intuition without a subscription. Neither is mandatory,
but either is a fast way to make the terms below feel less abstract.

      <td>Null-sec</td>

      <td>
        Null-security space: regions with no automated in-game police

      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="corporation">
      <td>Corporation</td>
      <td>EVE Online</td>
      <td>
        A player-run organisation --- this course's rough equivalent of a
        guild, and the base unit alliances are built from.
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="alliance">
      <td>Alliance</td>
      <td>EVE Online</td>
      <td>
        A coalition of corporations, usually formed to hold and defend
        null-sec territory together.
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="hangar">
      <td>Hangar</td>
      <td>EVE Online</td>
      <td>
        A corporation's shared item storage. Which members can see or take
        from it is a permission the corp's leadership sets.
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="blueprint">
      <td>Blueprint</td>
      <td>EVE Online</td>
      <td>
        An item that specifies what a manufacturing job consumes, and how
        long it takes, to produce something.
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="moon-mining">
      <td>Moon mining / moon-goo</td>
      <td>EVE Online</td>
      <td>
        Mining the rare ore compounds ("goo") found on certain moons --- the
        main scarce input to the game's higher-tier manufacturing.
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="ore-rarity-tiers">
      <td>R4&ndash;R64 (ore rarity tiers)</td>
      <td>EVE Online</td>
      <td>
        The rarity ladder for moon-mined ore, from common (R4) to rarest
        (R64), where each tier is roughly half as common as the one before
        it.
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="xenotime">
      <td>Xenotime</td>
      <td>EVE Online</td>
      <td>One of the rarest (R64-tier) moon ores.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="dysprosium">
      <td>Dysprosium</td>
      <td>EVE Online</td>
      <td>
        The material Xenotime refines into --- an input Tech II
        capital-ship construction can't substitute away from.
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="tech-ii">
      <td>Tech II (T2)</td>
      <td>EVE Online</td>
      <td>
        The higher, more capable and more resource-intensive tier of ships
        and modules, above the game's base "Tech I".
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="grand-exchange">
      <td>Grand Exchange (GE)</td>
      <td>Old School RuneScape</td>
      <td>
        The game's central, game-run marketplace, where trades clear
        against a game-set guide price.
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="auction-house">
      <td>Auction House (AH)</td>
      <td>World of Warcraft</td>
      <td>The game's player-to-player marketplace for listing and bidding on items.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="dps">
      <td>DPS</td>
      <td>Cross-game</td>
      <td>Damage per second --- a standard measure of a character or item's combat output.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="ehp">
      <td>EHP</td>
      <td>Cross-game</td>
      <td>
        Effective health pool --- a standard measure combining raw health
        with defensive stats into one "how much damage can this actually
        absorb" number.
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr id="bot">
      <td>Bot / botting</td>
      <td>Cross-game</td>
      <td>
        Software playing the game automatically --- often to farm currency
        or items around the clock --- in place of a human player.
      </td>
    </tr>
