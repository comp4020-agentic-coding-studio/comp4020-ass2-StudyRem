# Policies and support

## Late work and extensions

Work submitted after its due date loses 5% of the available mark per day
late, up to five days, after which it is not accepted without an
extension. Extensions are granted for documented illness or other
serious disruption --- email the convenor before the deadline where
possible, and as soon as you're able to otherwise.

## Data sources and citation

Most assessments in this course ask you to work from real, published data
about a real virtual economy (EVE Online's Monthly Economic Reports,
World of Warcraft's Auction House, Old School RuneScape's Grand Exchange,
or a documented incident such as the Guiding Hand Social Club heist).
Wherever a real source is involved --- whether it's the one an assessment
names by default or a substitute you've agreed with the convenor --- cite
it by name and link in every submission. Using a data source without
citing it, or presenting invented numbers as if they came from a real
report, is treated as an academic integrity matter, not a formatting
slip. The exception is an assessment built around a toy dataset provided
in the session itself (Balance Patch Notes' cost/performance figures, for
instance) --- there's no real source to cite, because the exercise is
testing the technique, not your ability to source data.

## Use of AI tools

You may use AI tools to help research, model, or draft your submissions,
provided you declare that use and can explain and defend every number and
claim in your own words. An assessment that cites a real economic
incident or dataset is expected to be checkable against that source ---
"the model told me so" is not a substitute for a citation.

## Getting help

Questions about a brief or a spec belong on the course forum, where
everyone benefits from the answer. For anything personal --- extensions,
wellbeing, accessibility --- email the convenor directly; contact details
are on the [people page](/people/).
